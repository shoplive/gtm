Try this

From faster production to higher conversions,
use it for what matters to you

Short-form videos convert better than images, but finding the right scenes has always taken someone's time. Start from the goal that matters most to your team.

CASE 01 · Production efficiency

If you want to cut short-form video production time

Scrubbing through a 1 to 2 hour stream to find segments made even a single short-form video take a long time.

Try this

Start with AI picks: add the segments it suggests and click Send to Shortform. You'll go straight to the Shortform editor, no editing needed.

CASE 02 · Product page conversion

If you want to raise conversions on product pages

You know short-form videos help purchase decisions, but there was no capacity to make an explainer video for every product.

Try this

Choose a product in By product, find clips with AI scene search (e.g. 'Find where product A appears'), then send them to Shortform and add them to the product page, where they help customers decide to buy.

CASE 03 · Ad creatives

If you constantly need ad creatives

Ads and social media always need new creatives, but choosing which scenes would perform well came down to gut feeling.

Try this

Pick by Clicks, Chats, or Likes, then add stream subtitles and a background to create a 9:16 video and download it. Subtitles carry the message even in social feeds watched on mute.

CASE 04 · Getting more from live content

If you want to keep using past live streams

Once a carefully produced stream aired, it went unused except as a replay.

Try this

Add a key segment for each product and split them into cards to create multiple videos in one go. Keep using them in promotions, home feeds, and external ads.

AI suggests candidate segments based on stream data. Your team makes the final call.

How it works

From an ended stream to a published short-form video in four steps

Open Clip Maker, find and add segments, then send them to Shortform or turn them into videos.

STEP 01

Open Clip Maker after the stream ends

1-1
Open Clip Maker

In the admin's campaign list, select an ended live campaign and click the 'Clip Maker' button at the top. The analysis and the video open together on one screen.

1 2 3 4

Select segments screen · 3 segments added after picking the hoodie in the product list

① Left panel · Find segments with AI scene search, recommended segments, and the product list.
② Center area · Browse segment cards and add them. Set the order under 'Selected segments' below.
③ Right area · Preview, then edit or send straight to Shortform.
④ Timeline · Shows thumbnails of the whole stream, markers for added segments, and a data graph.
Note · Analysis complete notification

The stream is analyzed automatically when it ends, and an email goes to the account that created the campaign once it's done. The button in the email takes you straight to Clip Maker. To add recipients, contact ask@shoplive.cloud.

Note · Uploading long-form videos

Even without a live stream, you can upload a long-form video and make short-form videos with Clip Maker's AI picks and AI scene search.

STEP 03

Edit and create a video, or send to Shortform

At the bottom right of the Select segments screen, choose the option that fits your goal.

Note · Video & subtitle downloads

If you only need the files, click Video & subtitles on the Select segments screen. Choose video (mp4) or subtitles (srt) to download and use them in other editing tools.

STEP 04

Edit and create videos

Refine the frame and subtitles, then click Create video to render a 9:16 MP4 for each card. Screen settings apply to all cards.

4-1
Cards and preview

Each card becomes one video. Split segments across cards to create several videos at once, and click Add more segments to bring in more.
