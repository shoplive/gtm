Scenarios

Use cases

Not sure where to start?
Just pick the case closest to your brand and follow it in your next live stream!

Scenario A

Expert guest sessionA remote expert shares the value of the product

In a vintage record (LP) auction live stream, the main host introduces the album and explains in depth the value and musical character of its classic tracks. Meanwhile, a record collector based overseas joins on screen to add the fascinating backstory of how this original pressing became so rare. The two keep the mood lively with fun, easygoing conversation and answer viewers’ questions in real time.

Suggested screen layout
PiP layout (2 people). The main host presents the LP on the full screen while the expert collector adds the backstory from a small window in the top right.
Expected impact
When someone you trust explains every detail, it's easy to see why the product is great—and that naturally leads to purchases and bids.
Scenario B

Viewer participation sessionReal customers share their own reviews

A live stream introducing a new skincare product. A viewer who has already tried the product sends a join request. Once the main host accepts, that viewer appears on screen as a co-host and shares how it actually felt, along with their skin type and how long they've used it. They also answer other viewers' questions in chat, clearing up doubts on the spot for people still deciding.

Suggested screen layout
Spotlight layout (3 people). The main host demos the new skincare product on the large bottom screen while two viewers who have tried it share real reviews from the top.
Expected impact
Instead of the brand making the pitch, real customers share vivid stories that are far more convincing—and other viewers naturally join the conversation.
Scenario C

Influencer collab sessionA team launches a new product together

A launch live stream debuting a new product. The brand manager leads as the main host, introducing the product's features and launch offers, while 2–3 influencers in different cities—like Seoul, New York, and Tokyo—join as co-hosts. From their own spaces, each influencer wears or uses the product in their own style, showing on one screen how many ways the same product can be enjoyed.

Suggested screen layout
Grid layout (4 people). The brand manager introduces the new product from the top tile while influencers in Seoul, New York, and Tokyo show it in their own style in the other three.
Expected impact
Influencers and fans from all over the world come together in one stream, and that synergy doubles your promotional reach.

Features

Here are the key features

From join requests to screen layout to ending the session, the main host manages everything in the Studio app.

FeatureDescription
Receive join requestsTurn on co-host requests in the multi-host menu during the stream so viewers can send join requests.
Accept / decline requestsChoose who joins from the request list. Once accepted, they go live as a co-host instantly.
Layout settingsSet a default layout for each group size (2, 3, or 4 people) before going live.
Change layout / orderChange the layout and speaking order mid-stream, then preview and save.
Mute co-host micThe main host can mute a co-host's mic. Co-hosts can turn it back on themselves.
RemoveThe main host can remove a specific co-host from the stream.
End all at onceTurning off multi-host ends all co-hosts' participation at once.

Layouts by group size

As more people join, the screen splits automatically, and you can choose a preferred layout for each group size.

Tips for running it safely

With multi-host on, any viewer can send a join request. Follow these five tips to stream smoothly with only the people you want.

  • Turn it on only when needed, and off right afterKeeping multi-host on only while you're taking guests reduces unwanted requests.
  • If your guests are confirmed, share the link in advanceCheck nicknames in the request list and accept only the guests you’ve lined up.
  • Do a quick rehearsal with guests before going liveChecking camera angle, lighting, and sound in advance means fewer surprises mid-stream.
  • Handle issues right awayMute or remove just that co-host and keep the stream going.
Co-hosts should use earphones!This prevents speaker sound from feeding back into the mic and echoing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

QCan I invite only specific people?

Currently, when multi-host is on, any viewer can send a join request, and the main host chooses whom to accept. If you have guests arranged in advance, share the stream link first and accept only their requests.

QHow many people can go live together?

Up to 4 people, including the main host.

QWhich devices can I use?

It depends on your role.

  • Main host Host the stream from the Shoplive Studio app for iOS.
  • Co-host Join from Android or iOS. No app install needed. Join right from your mobile device.
QCan I use it when streaming with OBS or PRISM?

Multi-host works when streaming from the Studio app. It isn't available when streaming through OBS or PRISM alone.

QCan a co-host end the stream?

No. Only the main host can start and end the stream.

QIf the main host mutes a co-host, can the co-host unmute themselves?

Co-hosts can turn their mic back on themselves.

QWhat happens when I turn off multi-host?

All co-hosts leave at once, and the main host continues the stream solo.
