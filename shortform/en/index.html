4 WAYS TO DISPLAY

Same Shortforms, four ways to display them

The right option depends on where it sits in your store. You don't have to pick just one. Use them together across pages. Tap a card to read the details.

01 · Collection

Gather Shortforms
and lay them out

This is the most basic way to display Shortforms. Lay out a Shortform group from Admin, or Shortforms matching tag and brand conditions, in a grid or horizontal row. Tapping a card opens the full-screen player.

Layouts

Pick a layout that fits the page width and placement. The same set of Shortforms can appear in different layouts in several places.

Grid

Lays out Shortforms like tiles. Great for feed pages where customers keep scrolling down.

Horizontal

Scrolls horizontally in a single row. Perfect as a strip in the middle of your home or promotion page. It can also auto-scroll.

Horizontal + ProductsNEW

Products linked to each Shortform appear right below it. Customers can go from video to product in one tap.

Key features

Gather

Set the conditions and it fills itself

  • Display a Shortform group created in Admin as is
  • Auto-build from tag, brand, or category conditions
  • Add tag tabs on top so customers can browse by topic
Watch

Preview right on the card, tap to watch full-screen

  • Videos preview on hover or when they scroll into view
  • Tap to keep watching in the full-screen player
Style

Matches your store design

  • Title and description below or inside the card
  • Horizontal: choose arrows, progress bar, or auto-scroll
  • Product thumbnails as circles or cards

Setup

Two steps: choose which Shortforms to gather in Admin, then place them in your store.

1Admin

Create Shortforms and organize them with groups and tags

Create Shortforms by cutting live streams with Clip Maker or uploading videos, then link products. Build a Shortform group or mark them with tags to decide what to display.

2Your store

Choose the placement and layout

Decide where to display, such as home or promotion pages, and choose Grid or Horizontal. Also pick which set to show (group, tag, or brand).

3Your store

Fine-tune display options

Adjust title position, product thumbnail shape, and more to match your store design. Shoplive can help if needed.

Use cases

CASE 01Home

Want a Shortform strip on your home page?

Home banners are hard to update often, and new content gets buried.

Ways to use itPlace a Horizontal Collection mid-page on your home and auto-fill it with the 'New arrivals' tag. Tag a new Shortform and your home page updates itself.
CASE 02Promotions

Want to show promotion products in video?

Each promotion product has an explainer video, but they're scattered.

Ways to use itCreate a Shortform group for the promotion and use the Horizontal + Products layout. Products appear right below the video, making purchase easy.
02 · Product Preview

Follows the product page,
showing related Shortforms

A small window floats on the product detail page and follows the customer as they scroll. It appears only when a Shortform is linked to that product, and tapping it opens full-screen.

Key features

Automatic

Shows only Shortforms linked to the product

  • Finds and shows Shortforms linked to the product page being viewed
  • Match more precisely with SKU or product name
  • Stays hidden when no Shortform is linked
Sticky

Stays in view while scrolling

  • Keeps showing while customers read the product details
  • Place it in any of the four corners
  • Optionally let customers resize the window
Unobtrusive

Once closed, it stays away for a while

  • Set how long to wait before showing again after it's closed
  • Different window sizes per device (PC, tablet, mobile)
  • Leaves your existing page layout untouched

Setup

1Admin

Link products to Shortforms

When creating a Shortform, attach the product it features. This link is how product pages find their Shortforms.

2Your store

Add it to product detail pages

Add the call code once to your product detail template and it applies to every product page. Pass the SKU or product name for more precise matching.

3Your store

Set position, size, and close rules (optional)

Choose the corner, size per device, and how long to wait before showing again after close.

Use cases

CASE 01Product detail pages

Videos getting buried in long product descriptions?

Product detail images are long, and customers scroll past videos placed in the middle.

Ways to use itPlace the Product Preview at the bottom of the screen and the video stays in view no matter how far they scroll. One tap opens it full-screen.
CASE 02After a live stream

Want to attach live stream moments to the products you featured?

Live replays are long, and finding the part about a product is hard.

Ways to use itCut each product's segment with Clip Maker, make a Shortform, and attach the product. That moment then appears as a preview on each product's detail page.
03 · Showcase NEW

Drop a Shortform
right where you want it

Place a Shortform directly where your store wants it, such as the thumbnail slot on a product detail page. There's no fixed spot, so it works in product list tiles, the top of a detail page, brand pages, anywhere. If editing code is hard, Shoplive can handle placement with a store-specific script.

Key features

Any placement

No fixed spot

  • Replace the whole product thumbnail slot with a Shortform
  • Works in every tile of a product list
  • Appears wherever it's called: top of detail pages, brand pages, and more
Easy setup

We can set it up for you

  • One call places a Shortform where you want it
  • If editing is hard, Shoplive sets position and conditions with a store-specific script
  • When your store design changes, just update the script
Reliability

No Shortform, no change to your page

  • Swaps the slot only after the Shortform has loaded
  • If loading fails, the original image stays untouched
  • Turn Showcase on or off for each Shortform

Setup

1Shoplive

Enable Showcase

Showcase is enabled per store. Ask your account manager and a Showcase option appears in Admin Shortform settings.

2Your store · Shoplive (if needed)

Choose the placement

Call it directly where you want it, such as a thumbnail slot or list tile. If editing is hard, tell us the spot and Shoplive can add it to a store-specific script.

3Admin

Turn on Showcase for the Shortforms to show

Each Shortform has a Showcase switch. Only Shortforms with it on appear in Showcase, and the attached products decide which product pages they appear on.

Use cases

CASE 01Product detail pages

Want to show fit and feel right in the photo slot?

Photos alone don't convey fit, texture, or real use.

Ways to use itReplace the product thumbnail slot with Showcase. Customers watch the video right where they'd swipe photos, then go straight to the buy button below.
CASE 02Product lists

Want to catch the eye from the product list?

It's hard to make one product stand out among static thumbnails.

Ways to use itPut each product's Showcase in its tile. The Shortform plays inside the tile, and tapping it goes straight to the product page.

Key features

Built from conditions

One round icon per brand or tag

  • Brands use their logo as the icon
  • Tags and Shortform groups use the first Shortform's thumbnail
  • Icons with no Shortforms to show hide automatically
New Shortform alerts

A ring signals new Shortforms

  • Rainbow ring when there are unseen new Shortforms
  • Gray ring once seen and nothing new
  • Catches the eye again every time you upload
Two modes

Play instantly or show the list

  • Tap a circle to play full-screen right away
  • Or expand that condition's Shortform list below the circles, like tabs
  • Circle size and ring colors match your store design

Setup

1Admin

Choose how to group the circles

Decide whether circles are made per brand, tag, or Shortform group, and attach that brand or tag to your Shortforms.

2Your store

Choose where the Story goes

Decide where the icon row goes: top of home, brand pages, category pages, and more.

3Your store

Set the playback mode and look

Choose whether tapping a circle plays right away or expands the list below, and set circle size and ring colors.

Use cases

CASE 01Home · brand pages

Want to show what's new across many brands at a glance?

The more brands you carry, the more new Shortforms get buried on home.

Ways to use itPlace a Story with one circle per brand at the top of home. Only brands with new Shortforms get the ring, so they stand out again.
CASE 02Promotions · category pages

Want Shortform tabs customers can browse by topic?

Building and updating a separate Shortform section for every promotion is tedious.

Ways to use itMake circles from tags or Shortform groups and turn on the list view. The first circle's Shortforms open below, and tapping another circle switches the topic.
